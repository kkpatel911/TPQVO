A partner you can rely on

TPQVO is your
credentialing partner.

TPQVO is a credentials verification organization that aims at improving credentialing processes for healthcare organization clients by handling the data-intensive and often frustrating process of verifying health care credentials. With over 20 years of CVO industry experience in hand, TPQVO removes some of the pain of health care credentialing by helping clients meet accreditation standards at the same time treating medical staff members and applicants as our customers.

TPQVO provides credentials verification services to a broad range of healthcare organization clients including hospitals, ambulatory surgery centers (ASC), office-based surgical practice, networks, nursing homes, and medical practices.

We follow Joint Commission, NCQA, and AAAHC credentials verification standards as required by our clients. We are experts in managing and verifying physician and other healthcare professional credentials information. Our goal is to help our clients focus on what they do best—providing healthcare services. Our clients trust us to help make credentialing as frustration-free as possible. That's why TPQVO is your credentialing partner.
